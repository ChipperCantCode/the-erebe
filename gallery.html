Photo guidelines
  • Share photos you took, or ones the photographer has said you can share, and credit them.
  • Ask before posting people. If someone is clearly recognizable, make sure they're OK with being online. Consent is a core part of burn culture.
  • Keep it all-ages: no nudity, nothing hateful or harassing, no ads.
  • Hidden location data (GPS) is stripped automatically from every photo when it's uploaded.
  • Photos stay the property of whoever took them. They're shown here only to celebrate the project.
  • In a photo and want it gone, or see your work posted without permission? Tap ⚑ on it. Photos reported for those reasons are hidden right away while we take a look.
