Help bring the Erebe to Black Rock City

The Erebe was built by volunteers for YOUtopia 2026, and it will take volunteers to bring it to the playa in 2027. We need hands for structural upgrades and repairs before the event, drivers with trucks and trailers, and a build crew to install it in Black Rock City. Carpenters of all skill levels are welcome.

We've applied for a Black Rock City Honoraria grant, and plans will firm up as decisions come in this winter. Sign up below and tell us how you'd like to help, and we'll be in touch.
